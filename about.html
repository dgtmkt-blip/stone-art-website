About the Product

A thin slice of genuine stone

Stone Art veneer is created by carefully separating a fine layer from natural slate and quartz. The result retains the exact colour, grain and texture of the parent stone — because it is the stone — while weighing only a fraction of a conventional slab.

This combination of the fresh characteristics of natural stone with a flexible backing confers pure elegance and sheer beauty, letting you clad walls, furniture and curved surfaces that solid stone could never reach.

Manufacturing

Craft meets consistency

Each Stone Art sheet is produced with care to preserve the natural surface of the stone while bonding it to a strong, flexible backing. This backing gives the veneer its bend, its resilience and its adhesion to a wide range of substrates.

The standard sheet size is 610 × 1220 mm (2 × 4 ft), giving designers a generous, workable format that can be cut and shaped to fit almost any application.
