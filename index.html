Who we are

Real stone, thin enough to go anywhere

Stone Art is a premium natural stone veneer crafted by Panelart Decor Pvt Ltd. By peeling a fine layer from genuine slate and quartz, we create surfaces that carry the authentic texture and character of natural stone — yet remain remarkably light and easy to apply.

From residential feature walls to large-scale commercial facades, our veneers give architects, designers and homeowners the freedom to work with real stone on virtually any surface.
